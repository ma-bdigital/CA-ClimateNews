# CA-ClimateNews — Licensing

This release is licensed in three parts, matching what the authors do and do not own:

| Component | License |
|---|---|
| **Annotations / labels** (L1, L2, adjudicated gold, IAA labels, guidelines) | **CC BY 4.0** — see `LICENSE-DATA.txt` |
| **Code** (scrapers, split builders, baseline scripts) | **MIT** — see `LICENSE-CODE.txt` |
| **Headline & lead text excerpts** | Not relicensed — © the respective news outlets; redistributed as short fair-use excerpts with source URLs and access dates. Takedown on request. |

All items are keyed to their **source URL and access date**, so the text can be
re-fetched from the original outlet ("rehydration") instead of relying on the
redistributed excerpts.

**To reuse:** cite the paper (CC BY 4.0 attribution) for the labels; the MIT notice
applies to any code you reuse. Do not treat the excerpted headline/lead text as
CC BY / MIT — it remains under its outlet's copyright.

**Takedown:** an outlet may request removal of its excerpts; contact the authors and
the affected rows will be replaced with URL-only entries.
